Tips for good panel photos
  • Safety first: follow your site rules; don't open or remove covers on live equipment just for a photo.
  • Open the door fully and stand in front of the middle of the plate.
  • Hold the phone upright and follow the green level dot.
  • Let the plate fill the screen but keep a small border so all 4 corners show.
  • No flash: it makes glare on covers and duct lids. Use the torch button or room light.
  • Glare on a cover? Step a little to the side: the app straightens the angle afterwards.
  • Wipe the lens; a fingerprint makes every photo soft.
  • After the whole panel, switch to Label close-up for nameplates you want to read later.
